Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Introduction

AUTUM LUXE ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website at www.aurumghana.com (the "Site"), place an order, or create an account. By using the Site, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, password hash, and delivery address when you register or update your profile.
  • Order Information: Products purchased, order history, payment information (processed via Paystack — we do not store card details), and delivery details.
  • Device & Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited.
  • Cart & Preferences: Items saved in your cart and wishlist (stored locally on your device and synced when logged in).
  • Communication Data: Records of emails and messages exchanged with our support team.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: create and manage your account; process and fulfill orders; send transactional emails (confirmations, shipping updates, password resets); send marketing communications (only with your explicit consent); respond to enquiries and support requests; detect and prevent fraud; improve the Site; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Payment Processing (Paystack)

All payment processing is handled by Paystack, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We do not store, transmit, or process your debit/credit card details on our servers. When you enter payment information, it goes directly to Paystack over an encrypted connection. Please review Paystack's Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

  • Session Cookies: Required for the site to function (maintaining your login session). Essential — cannot be disabled.
  • Local Storage: Your shopping cart may be stored locally on your device to improve performance. Not shared with third parties.
  • Analytics Cookies: Third-party analytics tools may collect anonymised data about pages visited and device type.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect Site functionality.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with logistics partners (courier companies to fulfill deliveries), Paystack (for payments), email service providers (bound by confidentiality), and law enforcement when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6.5. Dropship Suppliers

For orders containing dropship products, your name, delivery address, and order details are shared with our supplier partners to enable direct shipment. These suppliers are contractually bound to handle your data securely and only for order fulfillment purposes. We do not share payment information with dropship suppliers.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Account data and order history are retained for at least 5 years for accounting and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time — see Section 11.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including: passwords stored as bcrypt hashes (never in plain text); JWT-based authentication with short-lived access tokens; HTTPS encryption on all communications; rate limiting and input sanitisation to prevent injection attacks; and access controls limiting who can access user data within our team. While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to: Access your personal data; Correct inaccurate data; Delete your account (subject to legal retention); Withdraw consent for marketing; Data portability (machine-readable format); and Object to certain processing. To exercise any rights, email hello@aurumghana.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Third-Party Services

This Privacy Policy applies only to Aurum Ghana. The Site may contain links to third-party websites (social media, Paystack, logistics providers). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal data.

11. Children's Privacy

The Site is not intended for persons under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 18, we will delete it promptly. Contact us at hello@aurumghana.com if you believe a child has provided us with personal data.

12. International Data Transfers

If you are located outside Ghana, your data may be transferred to and processed in Ghana, which may have different data protection laws. By using the Site, you consent to such transfers.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
