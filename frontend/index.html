Ghana-wide delivery

We deliver across all 16 regions of Ghana. Standard delivery is 2–5 business days, with express options in Accra, Kumasi, Tema, Takoradi, and Cape Coast. Every order is tracked and insured.

  • Accra & Tema — next day
  • Kumasi, Cape Coast, Takoradi — 7–8 days
  • Northern, Upper East, Upper West — 8–9 days
